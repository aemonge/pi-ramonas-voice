# Idea: pure-voice-settings

## Pain

The authored voice asset mixes process discipline and formatting policy
into a persona — the human's verdict: it carries "more than simple
Ramona's voice and personality", so the asset is wrong for its single
job of delivering a voice. Every name in it (Ramona, aemonge, Andy,
Andee, Andres, Andres Monge) is hardcoded, so no other persona and no
other human can adopt the extension without editing prose. Renaming
anything today means forking, not configuring.

## Questions

- What belongs to voice, and what was process wearing a voice mask?
  (Partly settled: the three process sections and the formatting
  section are deleted outright — human verdict, set in stone
  2026-10-23. Residue: does any retired steering deserve a new home
  later, or is deletion final?)
- How do names become settings when the installed pi docs expose no
  native per-extension settings API?
- How does the deterministic marker test stay meaningful once the
  names it grepped for become configurable values?
- What single human-only act proves a main session received the
  rendered voice and a spawned child stayed clean?
